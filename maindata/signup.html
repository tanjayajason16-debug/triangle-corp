How to Sign Up for Triangle Corp

Triangle Corp isn’t an ordinary platform. To join, you must already be recognized as a known hacker in respected security circles.

1. Eligibility

  • Verifiable experience in ethical hacking or cybersecurity research.
  • Your alias should already be known within the community—no anonymous applications.

2. Invitation Process

  • No public registration.
  • If Triangle Corp deems you worthy, you’ll receive an official email invitation with private sign-up instructions.
  • Invitations are single-use and expire after 72 hours.

Terms of Service

  1. Purpose: Triangle Corp is for research, security testing, and collaborative problem-solving.
  2. Acceptable Use: Members must practice responsible, ethical hacking. No unauthorized intrusion or malicious exploitation is permitted.
  3. Confidentiality: All internal resources and discussions are confidential and may not be shared externally.
  4. Termination: Membership may be revoked at any time if rules are violated.
